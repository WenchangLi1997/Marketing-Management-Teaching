# Week 3 Brief: Embedding-Based Segmentation

Traditional segmentation often starts with a structured matrix of customer ratings. Embedding-based segmentation starts with unstructured language such as interviews, reviews, support conversations, or open-ended survey responses.

## Suggested workflow

1. Define the marketing decision the segmentation should support.
2. Clean and de-identify the text.
3. Convert each response into an embedding vector.
4. Cluster the vectors and inspect representative responses near each cluster center.
5. Name segments only after reviewing the underlying customer language.
6. Test stability, usefulness, reachability, and commercial relevance.

## Discussion questions

- What meaning does the embedding preserve, and what context may it lose?
- Are clusters driven by needs, sentiment, writing style, or accidental features of the data?
- Can the organization identify and serve the resulting segments?
- How should analysts combine qualitative judgment with quantitative validation?

This brief introduces the workflow. Instructors should add the specific embedding provider, privacy policy, and evaluation protocol used in class.
