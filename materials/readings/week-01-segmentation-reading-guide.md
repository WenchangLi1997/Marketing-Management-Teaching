# Week 1 Reading Guide: Segmentation Concepts and Case

Use these questions while reviewing the customer heterogeneity slides and case.

1. Which customer differences represent needs, and which variables only describe customers?
2. What evidence suggests that a preference is latent rather than absent?
3. How does the proposed segmentation improve on a broad market definition?
4. Which segments are attractive, and where does the organization have credible competitive strength?
5. Can the selected target be identified and reached in practice?
6. What should the positioning statement say about who, what, and why?

## Case preparation

For the DentMax example, compare the Loyalists, Switchables, Generalists, and Apathetics. Recommend one priority segment and identify the evidence that supports your choice.
