# Week 2 Case Guide: DentMax

## Preparation

Review the four customer segments and identify the segment whose needs best match DentMax's current strengths.

## Team task

Prepare a five-minute recommendation that covers:

1. The priority target segment.
2. The customer need that should anchor the strategy.
3. DentMax's relative advantage.
4. The evidence needed to validate the decision.
5. A one-sentence positioning statement.

## Positioning template

For **[target customer]**, DentMax provides **[primary benefit]** because **[credible reason it performs better than alternatives]**.
